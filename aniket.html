Facts about nutrients

  1. Carbohydrates are essential, serving as the body's primary energy source. Whole grains, fruits, and vegetables provide fiber and nutrients.
  2. Proteins build and repair tissues, support muscle growth, and aid immune function. Sources include meat, beans, and nuts.
  3. Healthy fats are necessary, supporting brain health and hormone production. Avocados, nuts, and certain oils are beneficial.
  4. Vitamins and minerals are crucial micronutrients that support immunity, bone strength, and metabolic processes. Examples include vitamin C, calcium, and iron.
  5. Water makes up about 60% of the human body, playing a vital role in digestion, circulation, and temperature regulation.
