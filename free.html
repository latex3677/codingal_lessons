About him

Monkey D. Luffy, commonly known as "Straw Hat Luffy" , is the founder and captain of the Straw Hat Pirates.
He pursues the legendary treasure of the late Gol D. Roger in order to become the new Pirate King and reach a further, untold dream.
He believes that being the Pirate King means having the most freedom in the world.

Why he is my favourite character?

Luffy is cheerful, carefree, and fiercely loyal. He values freedom above all and becomes serious when his friends are threatened.
His simple-mindedness hides strong instincts and ingenuity in battle.
